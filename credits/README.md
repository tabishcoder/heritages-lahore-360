### What was changed

- **badshahi-minar.webp**: removed purple colour fringing on the white domes, light noise
  reduction, opened up dark shadows, warmer colour balance, sharpening, 1.5× upscale. The soft
  Minar-e-Pakistan was replaced: its platform was cut from the second photo (taken on the same
  evening flight), perspective-warped onto the original platform and blended in, and the tower was
  cut out, scaled to the original's height and placed on top.
- **hazuri-fort.webp**: the second photo was aligned onto the first and blended in, then the same
  colour, noise and sharpening clean-up.
- Both are stored as partial panoramas so the 360° viewer shows them with correct perspective.
